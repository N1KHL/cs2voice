CS2 Voice

Extracts the players' voice from CS2 demos, so you can listen per round and per team and send clips and a report to FACEIT.

Free and open source, under the MIT License.

The idea came from csgo-voice-extractor by akiver.

Built with demoinfocs-golang, WebAssembly, opus-decoder (Opus), and wasm-media-encoders (LAME).
